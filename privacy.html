Fitzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

The short version

Fitzle does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party services.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress (current game, journey level, and settings) is saved only in your browser's or device's local storage. It never leaves your device, and we have no way to access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.

Network access

The app works fully offline. Network access is used only to load the app itself and receive updates from our web host (GitHub Pages). We run no servers of our own and receive no information about you or your play.

Children

Because Fitzle collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date above.
